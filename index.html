TalkReal

Sound natural. Speak with confidence.

An app that helps language learners sound conversationally natural by giving instant feedback, suggested improvements, and study tips focused on real talking.

View Deliverables Watch Concept Video

About the Project

The Problem

Speaking is the universal measure for whether you "know" a language, but it's also one of the hardest skills to practice. Learners need consistent conversational partners and the confidence to speak even though they might sound "incorrect." We need to bridge this gap.

Our Users

Language learners who want to sound conversationally natural but don't have regular chances to speak, or feel too nervous to try. This is especially true for early-to-intermediate speakers who care more about sounding natural than being perfect.

Our Solution

An app where you conversationally speak with AI, and it tells you whether you sound conversationally natural, offering pointed improvements and specific study tips targeted towards talking in real-world scenarios.
